# FoodHub NYC: Strategic Data Analysis & Operational Roadmap

## 📋 Executive Summary
This project analyzes the NYC food delivery ecosystem to optimize logistics and customer satisfaction. By decoding delivery patterns and consumer psychology, I identified a "Quality-Time Paradox" and validated the platform's rating integrity, providing a roadmap for high-margin growth.

## 🚀 Problem Statement
The NYC restaurant scene is more competitive than ever. For FoodHub, the challenge is to move beyond "moving boxes" and start optimizing the ecosystem. This analysis digs into the data to find out what actually drives a 5-star experience: Is it speed, consistency, or the "logistical tax" of a New York weekday?

## 🎯 Objectives
* **Behavioral Deep Dive:** Analyze demand patterns and identify true revenue-driving cuisines.
* **Operational Benchmarking:** Pinpoint whether delays happen in the kitchen or on the road.
* **Customer Sentiment Analysis:** Decode the "Silent Majority" and the psychology of ratings.
* **Actionable Strategy:** Provide data-backed recommendations to scale in a crowded market.

## 💡 Key Insights (Conclusions)
1. **The Weekday Tax:** Latency is 100% an external transit issue. Kitchen prep remains flat (~27m), but delivery times spike on weekdays.
2. **The Quality-Time Paradox:** High-cost orders ($25+) delivered *too fast* (<40m) actually see **lower ratings ( see 4.2.2)**. Customers link "waiting" with "hand-crafted quality."
3. **Revenue Matrix:** American/Japanese are volume "Cash Cows," while French/Thai are high-margin "Boutique" leaders.
4. **Operational Resilience:** Top brands like **Shake Shack** and **TAO** maintain efficiency even during massive weekend surges.
5. **Rating Integrity:** The 38% "Not Given" ratings are missing at random; their logistics mirror 5-star orders, proving the 4.34 platform rating is honest. (I’m especially proud of the deeper analysis I did here in **4.2.6** regarding the consistency of unrated orders.)
## 🛠️ Strategic Recommendations
### 1. Revenue & Growth
* **The Premium Pivot:** Launch weekend "Luxury Windows" for High-AOV cuisines (Thai/French).
* **Dynamic Tiering:** Award "Premier Partner" status based on consistency (IQR) rather than just speed.

### 2. Logistical Optimization
* **Weekday Routing:** Prioritize bike/electric vehicle deliveries during 5 PM – 7 PM peaks to bypass gridlock.
* **Dynamic ETA Padding:** Use accurate "Peak ETAs" for high-variance brands to manage expectations.

### 3. Customer Experience
* **The "Golden Window":** Calibrate algorithms to target a **40–50 minute** arrival—the perception "Sweet Spot."
* **Monetizing Silence:** Implement a "Review for Credits" program to capture data on average orders.
